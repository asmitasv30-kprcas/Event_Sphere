Our Story

From one candle-lit mehendi to a global stage.

It began on a humid August evening in 2023. Our founder Ishaani — a former theatre director — was asked to "make a friend's wedding feel like a film." She turned a Bandra garden into a 1940s noir set. The bride cried. The guests danced till dawn. The phone hasn't stopped ringing since.

A decade later, Event Sphere is a full-service atelier with in-house lighting engineers, floral stylists, choreographers, drone pilots and a creative direction team that thinks in stories first, logistics second. We've produced events at Lake Palace Udaipur, Burj Al Arab Dubai, Santorini cliffs and a glacier in Iceland — but we still treat every brief like that first wedding.

Because to us, an event isn't a transaction. It's a chapter in someone's life. And we'd rather write it brilliantly.
